# Email Correspondence Templates

This section contains ready-to-use email templates for course communication. Simply copy and paste the appropriate template, customize the details (dates, times, repository URL), and send to your students.

## Available Templates

### 📧 [Course Invitation Email](course-invitation.md)
Initial invitation for students with Git access who need to pull the latest repository updates and prepare for the first meetup.

### 🚀 [Pre-Course Setup Email](pre-course-setup.md)
Detailed setup instructions for students to prepare their development environment before the course begins.

### 📅 [Weekly Module Announcement](weekly-module.md)
Template for announcing new modules and weekly assignments.

### 🎯 [Midcourse Check-in](midcourse-checkin.md)
Template for checking student progress and providing encouragement.

### 🏆 [Course Completion](course-completion.md)
Congratulations email for course completion with next steps and career guidance.

## Customization Guidelines

### Required Customizations
- **[REPOSITORY_URL]**: Replace with your actual Git repository URL
- **[PROFESSOR_NAME]**: Your name and title
- **[COURSE_DATES]**: Specific dates for your course offering
- **[MEETING_TIMES]**: Your scheduled class/meetup times
- **[CONTACT_INFO]**: Your email and office hours

### Optional Customizations
- Add your university branding
- Include specific assignment due dates
- Customize tool recommendations based on your preferences
- Add local resources or support information

## Email Best Practices

### Subject Line Guidelines
- Keep subject lines clear and actionable
- Include course identifier (e.g., "DDD Course:")
- Use action words when appropriate

### Content Guidelines
- Keep emails concise but informative
- Include clear action items with deadlines
- Provide links to relevant resources
- Use encouraging and professional tone

### Follow-up Strategy
- Send reminder emails 24-48 hours before deadlines
- Follow up with students who haven't completed setup
- Provide additional support for struggling students
